About

Biography

Feng Hong is a professor and Ph.D. supervisor at Ocean University of China. His research interests include intelligent sensing, trajectory data mining, and marine data analytics. He has served as principal investigator on multiple projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and has also contributed to major national research initiatives, including the National Key R&D Program, the 973 Program, and the 863 Program.

He has authored or co-authored more than 100 publications in leading journals and conferences, including ACM Computing Surveys, Ocean Engineering, UbiComp, CHI, INFOCOM, SenSys, IJCAI, EWSA, and Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence.
